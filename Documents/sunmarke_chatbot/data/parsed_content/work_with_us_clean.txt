Life at Sunmarke is an exciting journey in an organisation that believes in transforming children’s lives.

Our teachers strongly believe in our education philosophy and play the role of nurturing adults who help spark imagination, build self-esteem in a child and help them discover their potential.

At Sunmarke, you will join a large family of some of the best teaching talent in the region, handpicked for their inspirational teaching.

You will be given the support, dedicated time, and world-class coaching and professional development to allow you to develop your skills and expertise to the highest levels, and keep abreast of the latest developments in teaching.

As we grow our schools, you will be given multiple opportunities to take on more senior roles and further your career.

You’ll love our warm and collaborative work environment, where transparency, camaraderie, and professional and personal growth are a way of life.

Come, join us and make a difference in your life and people’s lives.

Request payment of any kind from prospective candidates for employment or any sort of fees in return for a job at Sunmarke School

However, sometimes the recruitment process is done through professional recruiting agencies. In such cases, offers are always made directly by Sunmarke School and not by any third parties

Sunmarke School will not be responsible for anyone acting on an employment offer not directly made by Sunmarke School.

Our hiring & selection process involves interviews and assessments before any offer is made to a candidate.

The Sunmarke School Human Resources team does not request or require personal documents like bank account details or credit card information as part of the recruitment process.

Anyone making an employment offer in return for money or another type of gain is not authorised by Sunmarke School and is not offering an approved job.
