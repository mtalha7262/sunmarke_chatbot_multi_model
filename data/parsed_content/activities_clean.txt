Sunmarke offers over 160 extracurricular activities , including clubs for sports, performing arts, drama and theatre, journalism, music, maths, science, robotics, debate, public speaking, and more. These activities are designed to nurture children’s social, physical, emotional, and cultural growth, while helping them discover their passions, develop teamwork, and build leadership skills.

All activities are included as part of the school experience , giving students the freedom to explore and participate without additional costs. We encourage our students to take part in at least four ECAs per term to ensure a well-rounded and engaging school experience.

Please note that all listed free ECAs are subject to availability on a first-come, first-served basis and may change from term to term. Bookings are made through SchoolsBuddy , and for more information, parents can contact our Activities Manager, Anish Melroy Carl , at a.carl@fortes.co .
