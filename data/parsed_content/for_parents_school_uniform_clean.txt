Visit our Sunmarke Online Uniform Store to select and order uniform items and/or accessories. You may pay online and have the items delivered or pick them up from the Uniform Shop at the school building.

Students must be neatly dressed in ironed clothes and maintain a clean appearance.

Extreme hairstyles and colours are not allowed.

Students wearing trousers must wear a black belt.

Parents should provide an apron for Art & Craft class.

Shoulder length hair must be tied up for swimming and other practical lessons such as Art and Crafts.

Colourful nail polish is not allowed.

Students must wear black leather shoes and dark blue socks.

For safety reasons, shoes should be flat with closed tops and laces or Velcro straps.

Heels and boot-style shoes are not allowed.

All items of uniform must be marked with your child’s name.

Jewellery is not permitted for students with the exception of small stud earrings for girls or jewellery which is required to be worn for religious purposes.

These must be either removed or covered with tape by the parent on the day of PE.

Students of Key Stage 2 and above are allowed and encouraged to wear a watch to school.
